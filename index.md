# About me
I am an MSCA Postdoctoral Fellow at UiT The Arctic University of Norway in Tromsø, in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics and associated to the Lie-Størmer Center. Since September 2026, I have been working on the project PRISM, hosted by [Cordian Riener](https://www.cordian.de/). I am on leave from my position as assistant professor (non-tenure-track) at the University of Klagenfurt, where I work with [Angelika Wiegele](https://me.aau.at/~anwiegel/).

I obtained my PhD from Tilburg University in 2022 as part of the [MINOA project](https://minoa-itn.fau.de/), under supervision of [Etienne de Klerk](https://sites.google.com/site/homepageetiennedeklerk/) and [Monique Laurent](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~monique/). My thesis is [Symmetry Reduction in Convex Optimization with Applications in Combinatorics](/assets/pdfs/Thesis.pdf).

A more detailed [CV](/assets/pdfs/CV.pdf) is available online.

## Research interests
I study conic optimization for highly symmetric problems, investigating computational and analytical ways to exploit symmetries. In particular, I am interested in
* Semidefinite and polynomial optimization,
* Symmetry reduction and representation theory,
* Algorithms for conic optimization, and
* Computer-assisted proofs in extremal combinatorics and discrete geometry, including flag algebra methods.

## PRISM
My [MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships), **PRISM: Graph Profiles via Invariants, Sums-of-squares and Moment methods**, develops symbolic and semidefinite methods for certifying boundaries of polynomial images and graph profiles. The project combines polynomial optimization, real algebraic geometry, representation theory and extremal combinatorics. It started on 1 September 2026 at UiT, with a planned four-month secondment with Oleg Pikhurko at the University of Warwick.
